# Russian Website Texts

## Overview

Write for the page's purpose and reader, not for a generic "good text" shape. Preserve facts, terms, quotes, links, structure, and the author's level of confidence; remove AI-like polish, filler, cliches, and invented claims.

## When to Use

Use for Russian website copy, landing/page sections, service descriptions, news, public academic summaries, business pages, SEO edits, and rewrites where the text must sound human.

Do not use for legal final review, medical advice, or fact claims that require a domain expert; verify sources and state uncertainty.

## Workflow

1. Identify purpose: inform, sell, explain, announce, instruct, or summarize research.
2. Identify audience and genre: applicant, client, specialist, broad reader, academic reader, internal business reader.
3. Mark fixed material: facts, numbers, names, dates, terms, quotes, links, headings, and required structure.
4. Rewrite only what may change: syntax, rhythm, word order, redundant phrases, and tone.
5. Read the result aloud. If a sentence sounds like a template, rewrite it.
6. Audit facts and language before output.

## Rewrite Rules

| Keep | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Meaning, arguments, facts | Word order, sentence length, rhythm |
| Professional terms | Bookish turns where neutral speech works |
| Author's voice and confidence level | AI-like smoothness and filler |
| Quotes and links exactly | Cliches, inflated endings, empty summaries |
| Original structure unless asked otherwise | Overlong or chopped sentences |

Avoid these phrases and their close variants:
`следует отметить`, `таким образом`, `в заключение`, `важно подчеркнуть`, `необходимо отметить`, `стоит отметить`, `безусловно`, `конечно`, `несомненно`, `в современном мире`, `в эпоху цифровых технологий`, `динамично развивающийся`, `комплексный подход`, `эффективные решения`, `с одной стороны... с другой стороны`.

Do not make every paragraph symmetrical. Do not turn connected prose into lists unless the source or user asks for a list. Avoid neat three-part enumerations when the source did not have them.

Do not "improve" meaning by adding degree or causality: `сразу`, `увереннее`, `быстрее`, `лучше`, `помогает добиться`, `особенно важны`, `повышает качество`, unless that exact idea is already in the source. Natural style is not permission to add benefits.

## Genre Tone

Academic or diploma text: clear academic Russian without bureaucratic padding. Keep terms and caution.

Abstract or reference text: neutral, informational, compact.

Speech: natural oral rhythm, shorter sentences, allowed starts with `А`, `Но`, `Потому что` when appropriate.

Business website text: concrete, direct, no decorative claims.

Journalistic or public page text: lively, specific, but without extra facts.

## Russian Public Site Language

Prefer Russian words when they are natural: `процесс продаж` instead of `sales workflow`, `адаптация пользователей` instead of `onboarding`, `клиентский опыт` instead of `customer experience`. Keep established terms when replacing them would distort meaning.

Reduce Latin script in public Russian text. If an unavoidable non-established English term remains, give a short Russian explanation when the page context allows, or ask the user if a definition is required.

Do not invent Russian-looking words. After drafting, scan for made-up terms and replace them with existing phrases.

## Fact Discipline

Do not add claims, benefits, dates, statistics, laws, or examples that are not in the source. If a claim should be checked, search current sources. Prefer primary documents, official pages, named organizations, and human-written materials; compare several sources when possible. Treat AI summaries, generated snippets, and anonymous SEO pages as weak sources.

When unsure, say what is unverified instead of smoothing over the gap.

## Self-Check

Before answering, verify:
- no banned opener or closing formula survived;
- no cliche replaced one cliche with another;
- no added evaluation, speed, certainty, or causality such as `убедительно`, `сразу`, `увереннее`, `уникальный`, `лучший`, `передовой` unless it was in the source;
- quotes, links, facts, and professional terms are unchanged;
- the text fits the audience and purpose;
- the Russian sounds real when read aloud.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Cleaning up" the text into generic marketing copy | Keep the author's tempo and concrete details |
| Removing Latin script but leaving anglicism cliches | Replace the whole phrase with normal Russian |
| Adding confident benefits | Preserve the original confidence level |
| Adding small intensifiers for flow | Remove them unless the source already says it |
| Leaving `комплексный`, `эффективный`, `динамичный` | Use a concrete action or remove the empty adjective |
| Rewriting quotes or links | Leave them exactly as provided |
